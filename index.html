04

In the workbench.

  1. Choose a seed. Bundled synthetic fixtures only. Each is validated and rejects atomically if malformed.
  2. Compare raw and accepted. Side by side, in integer cents and units.
  3. Trace lineage. T5's duplicate link and T6's reversal, row by row.
  4. Work the findings. Filter by rule, status or decision, then open the cited transaction, evidence, site window and rule text.
  5. Record a decision. Notes sit in a separate layer and never edit the source report.
  6. Export. JSON with rule version and a SHA-256 of the input, or a formula-safe CSV of findings.

05

What it cannot tell you.

  • Whether a patient, site or claim is 340B-eligible.
  • Whether a duplicate discount occurred. No Medicaid inference exists in this slice.
  • Anything about savings, repayment, purchasing or compliance.
  • Anything about real data. There is no file import; the seeds are invented.

It reconciles configured synthetic relationships and shows its work. Any real use would need independently sourced rules and qualified human review.
